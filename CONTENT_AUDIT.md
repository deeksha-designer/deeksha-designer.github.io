## Content mapped into the redesign

- Legacy: founded in 1980 as Gita Convent School; renamed Scholars Pride in 2016.
- Academic span: Pre-Nursery through Class XII.
- CBSE: school states affiliation since 1984 and Class XII upgrade since 1992.
- Mission, vision and school values: retained in concise form from the existing homepage.
- Existing themes: creative environment, holistic development and academic scholarships.
- Academic calendar, syllabus and examination resource links retained.
- Class X, Class XII and JEE result sources retained with their published years.
- Code of Conduct, transport rules and mandatory public disclosure documents retained.
- Official 2026–27 fee PDF linked directly from Admissions and the footer.
- Authentic imagery: only assets already published on the school’s official website are used.

## Content-needed before production

- Exact admission workflow, eligibility, class availability and enquiry ownership.
- Accessible HTML transcription of the current approved fee structure and applicable terms.
- Required admission documents and approved admission FAQs.
- Stage-specific curriculum, teaching approach, assessment and verified activities.
- Verified facility names, descriptions, educational use and photo captions.
- Scholarship eligibility, award values, conditions and renewal criteria.
- Current dated news, notices and events with publish/expiry metadata.
- Approved sports, arts, cultural and student-life programme details.
- Current results metadata beyond the existing source files.
- Campus-visit handling: enquiry request versus bookable appointment.
- Leadership profiles and any current statutory dates/identifiers.
- Form delivery destination, consent copy, retention policy and spam protection.

## Important live-site issues found

- The current navigation uses vague labels such as “Extras” and places admissions behind Contact Us.
- The “Mandatory Documents” parent link resolves to an invalid URL, although its child PDFs work.
- Several images have empty alternative text.
- Fees and key documents are discoverable only through navigation dropdowns/PDFs.
- News/notices are not exposed as structured, date-aware homepage content.

## Design direction

Taste Skill is the primary art-direction system. For this approved homepage-only iteration, the visual system is light, image-led and editorial: bold Montserrat typography paired selectively with Pinyon Script, warm cream `#FFF0E5`, refined crimson `#B21F3D`, white and charcoal, square media, and no resting shadows. Dark colour is reserved for the global footer rather than used as dominant homepage bands.

Reference principles translated—not copied—from Sancta Maria, Manaskriti and Shiv Nadar School:

- persistent, high-clarity admissions access;
- programme discovery organised around a child’s stage;
- authentic campus and student photography used as evidence;
- a dedicated leadership-message composition;
- useful parent resources and original result documents surfaced from the homepage;
- a clear footer that preserves statutory and school-resource access.

No internal page layouts are included in this iteration. Navigation destinations either move to relevant homepage sections or open existing official school resources.
